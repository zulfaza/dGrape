# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers managing applications, profile data, CV tailoring, and company research in one workflow.

## Product Purpose

applytrack turns a messy job search into a structured pipeline: store profile data, track applications, tailor CVs with AI, and learn from outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Focused, practical, exact.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-heavy SaaS dashboards, decorative cards, playful gamification, and spreadsheet-like clutter.

## Design Principles

- Keep workflows dense, readable, and fast.
- Make profile data reusable across CVs, tailoring, and applications.
- Prefer explicit structured inputs over free-form ambiguity.
- Surface actions where users need them, without extra ceremony.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-friendly interactions, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe UI.
